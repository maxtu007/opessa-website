Core Offering

OEM & ODM Production at Scale

We handle the production infrastructure for international beverage brands at every stage of growth — from early development runs through to high-volume export production.

Every engagement is managed from Taiwan, coordinated with clear international communication, and backed by certified production facilities that meet the compliance requirements of regulated markets worldwide.

Product Development

Packaging Solutions
Canning, labelling, and packaging across standard and custom formats — designed with international retail and regulatory requirements in mind. We coordinate across multiple formats to match your product and market.

Production Management
On-the-ground production oversight at every stage — ensuring consistency, quality adherence, and on-time delivery across every manufacturing cycle. You get visibility into progress without the complexity of managing it directly.
