Manufacturing

Production
Process

Six controlled stages from verified raw inputs to shipment-ready output — each one certified, each one counted.

01
Stage 01

Raw Material Sourcing

Vetted supplier network Incoming quality control Routine inspection protocols

Consistent quality starts at the source. We maintain established supply relationships to ensure a reliable raw material chain, backed by routine quality inspections and rigorous incoming material controls. No ingredient enters the production line without first passing our verification standards.

02
Stage 02

Bottle Blow Molding

250ml – 2000ml range 28mm & 38mm neck sizes HACCP certified FSSC22000 certified

Using advanced hot-fill lightweight blow molding technology, we produce a diverse range of bottle formats — from 250ml through to 2000ml — across both 28mm and 38mm neck sizes. Every bottle is manufactured within HACCP and FSSC22000 certified environments, ensuring structural integrity and food safety compliance from the start.

03
Stage 03

Three-in-One Filling

HEPA Class 10,000 cleanrooms Integrated wash–fill–cap Independent CIP system

Bottle washing, liquid filling, and cap sealing are executed as a single integrated sequence within HEPA Class 10,000 cleanrooms — maintaining a controlled, contamination-free environment at every step. Our independent CIP (clean-in-place) system supports consistent hygiene standards and flexible throughput across different bottle types and formats.

Precision filling at every stage.
04
Stage 04

Labeling

Single-head configuration Double-head configuration Client-defined throughput

Flexible labeling configurations — single-head and double-head options — allow clients to align production output with their specific volume and scheduling requirements. Accurate, reliable application across every production run, regardless of throughput scale.

05
Stage 05

Packaging

Carton packaging Shrink film packaging Full wrap & shallow tray

We offer carton and shrink film packaging across full wrap and shallow tray formats — giving clients the flexibility to match packaging style to product type and retail requirements. Both options are configured for efficient palletisation and international export readiness.

06
Stage 06

Logistics & Export

Cartons & shrink film Plastic crates & metal drums Load optimisation International export ready

From cartons and shrink film to plastic crates and metal drums, our logistics handling adapts to the requirements of each shipment. Load optimisation and smart arrangement are applied to every outbound order — maximising freight efficiency across all international destinations.
